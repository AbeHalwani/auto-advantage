Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, honest answers about our repair availability, shop hours, estimating, and turnaround times.

Can you take a look at my car?

Yes, absolutely. Our shop is open Monday through Friday from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. You can stop by anytime during open hours, or book a free estimate right here on our website.

What is your availability for an appointment?

We take appointments Monday through Friday from 9:30 AM to 4:30 PM. You can choose the exact day and time slot that works best for you right on our online booking form.

How much will the repair cost?

Every car and accident is different. Some cars just need small scratch touch-ups, while others need replacement panels or frame realignment. Looking at your car in person lets us write an exact, honest price before any work begins.

Do I have the right to choose my own repair shop in Michigan?

Yes, 100%. Under Michigan law, you have the full legal right to pick whatever repair shop you trust. Your insurance company cannot force you to use their shop. You choose us, and we handle everything directly with them.

Do you handle the insurance claim paperwork for me?

Yes, we do all the heavy lifting. We send photos and estimates to your insurance company, speak directly with their adjusters, and make sure all necessary repairs are approved and paid for without headache.

How does deductible financing work if I do not want to pay all at once?

If you have an insurance deductible like $500 or $1,000, we partner with Snap Finance and Sunbit so you can split that cost into simple monthly payments. You can pre-qualify in seconds with 0% interest options so you never have to delay repairs.

How long will it take to get the work done?

It is usually a couple days for parts to arrive, a couple days to fix the metal body panels, and a couple days to paint and bake the finish. Most repairs take between 5 to 10 business days from start to finish.

Do you give a warranty on repairs and paint?

Yes. All of our structural body repairs and computer-matched paint work come with a written lifetime warranty. For as long as you own your vehicle, our work is protected.
